## What follows on events.promptugal.news

1. `/sources/`: every source on data.promptugal.news's register, read and not read, each with its own anchor (`/sources/#<id>`): what it gives, why we do not read it (the quote, its basis, its link, the date), its licence limitations, whether we asked; and every host Perplexity cited that is not on the register yet. A note on top: tell us, and we change.
2. `/principles/`: our principles (accurate; never representing anything as other than it is; mistakes corrected as soon as possible, in the open) and the rules of engagement we follow with sources.
3. `/report/`: how to report a mistake or a concern, what happens next, and the corrections log.
4. Every page links the three from its footer; every event links `/report/`.
5. conductor.b@ is asked to make the principles and the report process PrompTugal-wide (every site), from these pages.
